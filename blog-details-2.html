5 Factors That Influence Property Valuation

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Property Valuation
Feb 03, 2026 David Chen Valuation

Understanding what drives property value is essential for buyers, sellers, investors, and property owners. Professional appraisers consider a wide range of factors, but five key considerations consistently have the greatest impact on market value.

1. Location and Accessibility

Location is often cited as the single most important factor in property valuation. Proximity to schools, shopping centers, transportation hubs, and employment opportunities can significantly increase value. The quality of surrounding neighborhood also plays a crucial role.

2. Property Size and Boundaries

The size of the land parcel, its shape, and its exact boundaries all influence value. An accurate boundary survey ensures you're assessing the true size of the property. Irregular or disputed boundaries can negatively impact marketability and value.

3. Condition and Use

The physical condition of any buildings and the current or potential use of the land (residential, commercial, agricultural) are major valuation factors. Well-maintained properties with versatile zoning command higher prices.

4. Market Conditions

Supply and demand in the local Real Estate market directly affect values. Market trends, interest rates, and economic conditions all influence what buyers are willing to pay at a given time.

5. Zoning and Legal Considerations

The zoning classification of a property determines what it can be used for, which significantly impacts its value. Properties with flexible zoning or potential for redevelopment are often valued higher than those with restrictions.
