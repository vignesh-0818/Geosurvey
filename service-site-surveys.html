Service Overview

A site survey records the existing conditions of a property, capturing boundaries, buildings, structures, utilities, roads, and natural features. This complete picture is essential for feasibility studies, planning, and design.

Our surveyors produce detailed site plans that inform architects, engineers, and developers before any design or construction work begins, avoiding surprises and supporting smooth project progress.

Key Benefits

Complete Picture

Full existing conditions recorded accurately.

Informed Planning

Data that supports design and feasibility.

Smart Decisions

Avoid surprises with reliable site data.

Multi-purpose

Plans useful across many project types.

What's Included

  • Boundary and site feature mapping
  • Building and structure measurements
  • Utility and service location
  • Access and easement identification
  • Vegetation and terrain notes
  • Digital and printed site plans

Site Survey Process

We perform comprehensive site surveys using a structured approach:

  1. Initial site assessment and access planning
  2. Boundary and existing feature identification
  3. Detailed measurement and data capture
  4. Utility and service location mapping
  5. Site plan preparation and review
  6. Digital and printed site plan delivery
