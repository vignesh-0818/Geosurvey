Why Every Property Owner Needs a Boundary Survey

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Boundary Surveys
Jan 12, 2026 Sarah Mitchell Boundary

Property boundaries define the physical extent of your land, but many homeowners have never had their boundaries professionally surveyed. A boundary survey is one of the most valuable investments a property owner can make, offering both legal protection and long-term financial security.

What is a Boundary Survey?

A boundary survey is a professional determination of the exact legal boundaries of a parcel of land. Certified surveyors use a combination of historical property records, deeds, aerial photography, and high-precision GPS equipment to establish where your property begins and ends.

Why It Matters

Without an accurate boundary survey, you risk unknowingly encroaching on a neighbor's land or having someone encroach on yours. Disputes over property lines are among the most common and expensive legal issues homeowners face, and they can often be prevented with proper documentation.

Legal Protection

A certified boundary survey provides legally recognized documentation of your property lines. This is particularly important if you're planning to install a fence, build an addition, or resolve a dispute with a neighbor. In many cases, the survey is a prerequisite for obtaining building permits.

Financial Value

An accurate boundary survey protects the value of your property. When it comes time to sell, having a recent certified survey can streamline the transaction, give buyers confidence, and potentially increase the sale price. It also ensures you're paying taxes on the correct amount of land.

When Should You Get One?

  • Before purchasing or selling a property
  • Before building a fence, wall, or structure near boundaries
  • When planning to subdivide or develop land
  • If you suspect an encroachment or dispute
  • When refinancing your mortgage
