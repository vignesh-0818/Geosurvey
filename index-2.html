Boundary Surveys

Determine the exact property boundaries of your land with professional accuracy. Our boundary surveys establish legal property lines, identify encroachments, and provide official documentation essential for property transactions, construction, and dispute resolution.

  • Legal boundary identification
  • Encroachment detection
  • Property line monuments

Aerial Drone Surveying & Photogrammetry

Leverage state-of-the-art unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) photogrammetry for large-scale topographic mapping, 3D volumetric analysis, and site inspection. Our FAA-certified pilots capture high-resolution georeferenced spatial data rapidly and safely, providing comprehensive surface visibility across difficult or expansive terrain.

  • High-resolution orthomosaic mapping and orthorectified imagery
  • Dense 3D point cloud generation and elevation surface models (DEM/DTM)
  • Cut-and-fill earthwork calculation and stockpile volumetric auditing
  • RTK-georeferenced flight paths with sub-centimeter ground control
