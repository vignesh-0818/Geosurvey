Last updated: January 1, 2026

1. Introduction

At GeoSurvey Pro, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information and land data you share with us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, utilize, and protect your information when you access our website or engage our land surveying and property valuation services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies you or relates to your property and inquiry, including:

  • Contact Details: Full name, email address, telephone number, and physical mailing address.
  • Property Information: Parcel numbers, deed records, site addresses, boundary descriptions, and requested survey types.
  • Technical and Field Data: Geodetic measurements, topographic elevations, aerial imagery, and digital CAD deliverables.
  • Website Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited on our site.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected to:

  • Perform requested land boundary surveys, construction staking, topographic mapping, and certified property valuations.
  • Communicate project timelines, status updates, consultation schedules, and report deliveries.
  • Ensure full compliance with state and national surveying boards and municipal regulatory filings.
  • Continuously improve the functionality, accuracy, and security of our online portals and services.

4. Data Protection & Confidentiality

All client data, property records, and proprietary survey files are protected with industry-standard encryption, strict access controls, and secure physical and cloud storage. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or property information to third-party advertisers or marketers.

5. Third-Party Disclosures

We only share information when legally authorized, such as with municipal recording offices, title agencies upon your instruction, or licensed survey partners directly engaged on your project under non-disclosure obligations.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections or updates, or inquire about the retention schedule of your survey documentation. For privacy inquiries, contact our Privacy Compliance Officer at privacy@geosurveypro.com.

Have Questions About Your Survey Data?

Our licensed professionals are here to assist you with transparent information and trusted guidance.
