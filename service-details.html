Service Overview

A boundary survey is a professional identification of the exact legal boundaries of your property. Our certified surveyors use GPS/GNSS equipment, total stations, and historical property records to establish precise property lines that are legally recognized.

Boundary surveys are essential for property purchases, real estate transactions, construction projects, fence installation, resolving neighbor disputes, and obtaining property development permits. An accurate boundary survey provides legal protection and peace of mind.

Key Benefits

Legal Protection

Certified boundary documentation prevents disputes.

Property Security

Know your exact property limits with confidence.

Property Value

Accurate boundaries increase property value.

Neighbor Relations

Avoid conflicts with neighboring properties.

What's Included

  • Research of existing property records and deeds
  • Field survey using GPS/GNSS equipment
  • Analysis of adjacent property boundaries
  • Identification of encroachments
  • Preparation of boundary survey plat
  • Official survey markers and monuments
  • Digital and printed survey documentation

Our Survey Methodology

Our boundary survey process follows a systematic approach to ensure accuracy and legal compliance:

  1. Review of title deeds, property records, and historical survey data
  2. On-site reconnaissance to assess conditions and access points
  3. High-precision field data collection using advanced equipment
  4. Data processing and boundary line computation
  5. Preparation and review of the final survey plat
  6. Official certification and client delivery

Accuracy & Certification

Every completed survey is signed, sealed, and certified by a licensed Professional Land Surveyor (PLS) registered in the jurisdiction where the work is performed. This certification serves as your legal assurance that the survey was completed with due diligence, in accordance with applicable standards, and reflects the professional judgment of a qualified surveyor.
