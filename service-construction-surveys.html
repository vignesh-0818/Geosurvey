Service Overview

Construction surveys provide the exact reference points, elevations, and layout controls that guide contractors through every stage of a build. From establishing building corners to monitoring ongoing works, we keep your project on position.

Our surveyors set out structures, roads, utilities, and site elements precisely against the design drawings, reducing errors, rework, and costly delays throughout construction.

Key Benefits

Accurate Set-out

Buildings and structures placed exactly on plan.

Reference Points

Reliable control for every stage of build.

Cost Control

Fewer errors and less costly rework.

Ongoing Support

Surveyors available throughout construction.

What's Included

  • Design drawing review and coordination
  • Primary and secondary survey control
  • Building and structural set-out
  • Drainage and utility layout
  • Level checks and monitoring
  • As-built verification reports

Construction Support Process

We support construction teams with a clear, dependable workflow:

  1. Review of design and construction drawings
  2. Establishment of primary survey control
  3. Setting out of building and site elements
  4. Monitoring and as-built verification
  5. Ongoing construction support visits
  6. Final as-built survey and report

Construction Support

Our construction surveyors work alongside contractors and project managers to deliver accurate set-out and reliable as-built data that keeps projects on schedule and within budget.
